# Engine calibration evidence policy
The current engine's output, knock and life estimates are **not calibrated** to measured dyno data.
The new calibration harness accepts three or more measured RPM / power-kW points and reports absolute percentage errors.
To claim empirical calibration: cite traceable test bench source, engine identity, conditions, power correction standard, dyno/measurement method, uncertainty and measured curve. Do not substitute manufacturer peak-rating figures for a full RPM dyno curve.
The synthetic unit test only verifies error arithmetic, not engineering accuracy.
Do not use estimated life hours for real-world maintenance, design or safety decisions.
