# Evidence registry: Robin EY20D dynamometer (candidate real measurements)
Source: *New Design of a Low Cost Small Engine Dynamometer for Engine Testing*, Applied Mechanics and Materials, volume 699, Table 4; https://www.researchgate.net/publication/286772651_New_Design_of_a_Low_Cost_Small_Engine_Dynamometer_for_Engine_Testing

Equipment listed in original: Robin EY20D gasoline engine, bore 67 mm, stroke 52 mm, displacement 183 cc, compression ratio 6.3, single-cylinder 4-stroke. Hydraulic pump dynamometer measurement; reported torque computed from pump pressure, power computed from torque and RPM.

3500 / 8.85 / 2.97
4000 / 8.02 / 3.36

DATA QUALITY WARNING: at 3000 and 3500 RPM the printed torque and power differ by over 5% from P=T*2*pi*rpm/60. These may be transcription, measurement or table inconsistencies. Do not silently alter measurements. Isolate these two points and document provenance. Other table points are rounded to 0.01.
The complete test load condition, exact fuel mixture, environmental correction, and volumetric/thermal efficiency are not stated well enough to select simulation inputs unambiguously. No empirical model calibration is claimed; this is a source-verified reference dataset candidate pending quality and condition matching.
